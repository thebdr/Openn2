# C-046 evidence - the SYCON.net GUI driver, its read-back oracle and the structure laws

## The oracle (2026-10-23 → 2026-10-25)
Every operation of the driver is judged by the saved `SYCON_net.xml` read back (`sycon5/readback.py`): the Device
elements (`syc:symbolicName`, `deviceNo`, `fdt:manufacturerId` / `deviceTypeId`, the `PositionRect` box, the BusLine
drop line) and the DTM `BinData` blobs (4-byte length + an OLE compound document, read with olefile 0.47: the
names, IPs, the slots' order numbers, the submodules, the parameter records by index, the controller's
`pn:DeviceList` and `DTMProxy` streams). The fixtures under `spike/references/E2E_Round1..3` pin the parser
(`tests/test_readback.py`); the driver rounds on the VM `Win11-Test` (SYCON.net 1.500, build 210311) assert the
read-back after every operation (`scripts/e2e_driver_round3.py`: a controller, an ET200SP with a module, a
parameter and a record, 5 minutes; then the builds of C-047).

## The laws and what taught them (`tests/test_architecture_contracts.py`)
- L2 the oracle apart: `readback` and `handoff` import nothing of the package. `canvas` logged through
  `session.log` until 2026-10-25 night - one import that pulled pywinauto into the XML side; the one-line `log`
  moved to `sycon5/log.py` (re-exported by `session`) and the law holds: readback, handoff, canvas, workspace and
  the GUI import without pywinauto, pywin32 or comtypes (`tests/test_package_import.py` hides them and imports).
- L3 the GUI sealed: the users' GUI (C-045) runs every step as a subprocess; it imports `workspace` alone - a
  build in the GUI's process would drive SYCON.net from a tkinter main loop, and a user's click would steal it.
- L4 one facade: 2026-10-25 22:56 a probe (`probe_xml_clone.py`) attached to the user's own running SYCON.net,
  opened its controller dialog, issued a Save (nothing written) and closed it. `SyconDriver.open` now refuses a
  running instance unless `attach=True`; the law keeps `Session`, `attach_if_running` and the dialogs behind
  `driver` so no other module can reach a running SYCON.net around that guard.
- L5 the tests never drive: the suite (31 tests, 3 s) runs on the fixtures on any machine; the driver is verified
  only on the VM, by hand-started rounds.
- L1 the direction: the import map of 2026-10-25 night, every edge pointing down -
  `canvas → handoff, log`; `workspace → handoff`; `session → log`; `catalog → drawing, session`;
  `dialog → session`; `exports → session`; `controller → dialog, session`; `device → dialog, drawing, session`;
  `driver → catalog, controller, device, readback, session`;
  `consumer → canvas, exports, catalog, driver, handoff, readback, session`; `gui → workspace`.

## Refute round 1 (2026-10-26; `tests/test_refute_c046.py`)
Attacked: the read-back on a DTM blob cut in half, a non-hex blob, a blob with a true length prefix and nothing OLE
behind it, a truncated XML, a Device without attributes or BusLine; the laws on synthetic sources (function-level and
relative imports, sideways and upward edges, constructions through a module attribute, calls by bare name, dynamic
imports, words against calls).
Refuted, fixed:
- a blob cut in half read as a device with its name and IP and no modules (olefile serves the directory that
  survived) - `readback.ReadbackError` now: the 4-byte length prefix must equal the body (true of every blob of the
  fixtures), a non-hex or non-OLE blob is named with its device;
- law L4's construction rule skipped `driver.SyconDriver()` (a module attribute) and a bare `attach_if_running()`
  was not a call to it - the rules are pure functions now (`constructions_in`, `calls_in`), tested on synthetic
  sources;
- law L5 was textual: it flagged its own docstring and the refute test's strings - `drives_in` walks the ast for
  Call nodes (a word in a string or a comment is not a call); a dynamic import (`importlib.import_module`,
  `__import__`) outside `__init__` is a violation now (the ast cannot see its edge).
Held: a truncated XML raises `ParseError`; the two fixtures read whole; the import walker sees every kind of import;
sideways is up; a Device without attributes reads as an empty device.
